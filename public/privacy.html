Privacy policy

Relic Vault · effective 27 September 2026

Relic Vault is a game about merging cursed treasure. It has no accounts and asks for no name, email address, contacts, photos or location. This page says what the game keeps, what it sends, who else sees data, and how to delete it.

What stays on your device

  • Your vault: your progress, settings and a record of what you bought. It is kept in your browser's or the app's own storage (IndexedDB, with localStorage as a fallback), not on our servers.
  • A shop log: the last 256 shop events (an offer shown, a purchase button pressed, a purchase), each with the item, what showed it, the session time and your rank, gems and gold. We use it to tune the game.
  • A random device id (rv_did): a random number made the first time you play. It is not your advertising ID, and it names nothing about you.
  • Your age band: under 16, 16 or over, or unknown, from the question the game asks at the start.

What the game sends

When a build has gameplay telemetry switched on, it sends gameplay events to a collector run for Relic Vault. Each event carries:

  • the event's name and time (for example a merge, a rank gained, an offer shown, a session started or ended);
  • the random device id rv_did and a random id for the session;
  • the app version, and the platform: your operating system family (such as Android or Windows, read from your browser) and whether the game is installed or running in a browser; in the Android app, the major version of Android System WebView, which runs the game;
  • the event's details: what was merged or bought and why it was offered, your rank, gems and gold, how long the session has run, your age band, and, if the game hits an error or a purchase or ad fails, the error's text and code.

We use these events to see where the game is confusing or unfair and to fix it. We do not sell them, and we do not use them to show you ads.

Google AdMob and Google Play

In the Android app, ads are shown by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and how you interact with ads, under Google's privacy policy (how Google uses data from apps that use its services). Purchases and installs go through Google Play, which processes your payment and account details under the same policy. We never see your card or your Google account.

Builds that show "AD · DEMO" veils, like the web playtest, contact no ad network, and their purchases are simulated and never charged.

Players under 16

If you tell the game you are under 16, it switches off random paid chests and shows you no sales offers. The shop still lists items at fixed prices. If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before you buy anything.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and tap Delete My Data twice. It erases everything above from your device: the vault, the shop log, the random device id, and any data an ad or store service keeps inside the game. The game then restarts as a new player with a new random id. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, does the same.

Events already sent are linked only to the old random id. To have those deleted too, write to us (below) before you delete, and tell us roughly when you played.

Contact

Write to the developer contact email shown on Relic Vault's Google Play listing.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the game's Settings links here.
