# Depth probe v2 — 2026-09-21 (RV-P2, AB#274)

The honest rebuild of the runway probe the Depth Critic called flattering. Code: hq `ops/probes/relic-vault-runway.ts`.
Game: relic-vault `main` at e88f664 (floors and the Chronicle shipped; the gem-chest odds of AB#380 are not in it, and
no bot buys anything anyway). Raw output: `2026-09-21-probe-v2.txt`. v1 re-run on the same build, for comparison:
`2026-09-21-probe-v1-rerun.txt`.

**Every number is in actions.** A minute figure is always the Board's measured pace, ≤ 1.18 s/action
(`2026-09-19-board-playtest.md`, Bug #307): an upper bound from one engaged player. Days: engaged = 1,271 actions/day
(25 min), casual = 610 actions/day (12 min).

## Re-stated: content runway (Antechamber only)

| Median of 12 | Living Relic (T9) | Last new relic | Longest gap without a new relic |
|---|---|---|---|
| focused, engaged | **1,155 actions** (≈23 min, day 0.9), 12/12 runs | 1,155 actions | **16,645 actions** (to day 14) |
| focused, casual | 1,155 actions (day 1.9), 12/12 | 1,155 actions | 7,391 actions (to day 14) |
| planner, engaged | never (T8 in 5/12 runs at 15,895) | 16,534 actions (day 13) | 6,025 actions |
| planner, casual | never (T8 in 1/12) | 7,355 actions (day 12) | 3,254 actions |
| greedy, either | never (not even T7) | 381 actions | the rest of the fortnight |

- **The Antechamber's content is consumed in under an hour by a player who goes for it.** The focused bot makes a
  Living Relic in 1,155 actions; the Board made one in ≤ 41 min (≤ ~2,080 actions at their pace). The bot is faster
  than the human upper bound, as a bot should be, and the two agree on the order of magnitude. v1 said a planner
  never makes one in 14 days; that described v1's planner.
- **The gate is rank, not skill.** The focused bot's Star-Metal Ark lands at 1,028 actions, just after rank 6 opens
  the Star Well at 1,012 — the Moon Sickle binding is the wall.
- **What comes after the Living Relic is not measured.** The next content is the floors (descend, restore, Keeper's
  Renown — docs/ROOMS.md), and no bot descends. So "16,645 actions without a new relic" is the length of the
  Antechamber's tail for a focused player, not the game's runway. A descending policy is the next probe story.

| Median of 12 | 14-day pass (target day 10-12) | Rush top milestone, first 4-day cycle (target: not before day 3) | Album page I (target ~day 3) |
|---|---|---|---|

- **An engaged patron-minded player finishes too early**: the pass at day 8.2 (target 10-12) and the Rush top at day
  2.8 of 4 (target not before day 3). Album page I is on time.
- **A casual 12 min/day player misses both**: the pass completes in 1 run of 12, and the Rush top milestone in none.
  That is RV-D6's (#275) question, and on this build the answer is no.
- A player who ignores patrons (as the Board did) gets neither pass nor Rush: both are fed by orders.

## Gold

## Blind class

Floors 2-10 (bots never descend), anything bought (gems, chests, passes), rewarded ads, and every human but one. The
greedy baseline is deterministic, which the probe checks each turn; a baseline that is deterministic and badly chosen
would still inflate or deflate density, and nothing here can see that.
