# Depth probe v2 — 2026-09-21 (RV-P2, AB#274; corrected by AB#379)

The honest rebuild of the runway probe the Depth Critic called flattering. Code: hq `ops/probes/relic-vault-runway.ts`,
bots in `ops/probes/relic-vault-sim.ts`. Game: relic-vault `main` at e88f664 (floors and the Chronicle shipped; the
gem-chest odds of AB#380 are not in it, and no bot buys anything anyway). Raw output: `2026-09-21-probe-v2.txt`. v1
re-run on the same build, for comparison: `2026-09-21-probe-v1-rerun.txt`.

**Corrected the same day (AB#379).** The first cut of v2 had two faults the value probe exposed: the bots never
collected rank rewards, the free pass or Rush milestones, and "gold earned" missed income spent on generator upgrades
in the same step. Both are fixed and every figure below is from the corrected run. What changed: the engaged planner
is **on time** for the pass (day 10.0, not the 8.2 first reported) and reaches the Star-Metal Ark in 12/12 runs, not
5/12; the casual player misses the pass in **every** run (not 1/12). The focused and density findings held.

**Every number is in actions.** A minute figure is always the Board's measured pace, ≤ 1.18 s/action
(`2026-09-19-board-playtest.md`, Bug #307): an upper bound from one engaged player. Days: engaged = 1,271 actions/day
(25 min), casual = 610 actions/day (12 min).

## Re-stated: content runway (Antechamber only)

| Median of 12 | Star-Metal Ark (T8) | Living Relic (T9) | Last new relic | Longest gap without a new relic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| focused, engaged | 1,019 actions, 12/12 | **1,138 actions** (≈22 min, day 0.9), 12/12 | 1,138 | **16,659 actions** (to day 14) |
| focused, casual | 1,019 (day 1.7), 12/12 | 1,138 (day 1.9), 12/12 | 1,138 | 7,405 (to day 14) |
| planner, engaged | 3,400 (day 2.7), 12/12 | 12,000 (day 9.4), **1/12** | 12,761 (day 10.0) | 8,623 (to day 14) |
| planner, casual | 3,400 (day 5.6), 12/12 | never | 5,433 (day 8.9) | 3,575 (to day 14) |
| greedy, either | never | never | 381 | the rest of the fortnight |

- **The Antechamber's content is consumed in under an hour by a player who goes for it.** The focused bot makes a
  Living Relic in 1,138 actions; the Board made one in ≤ 41 min (≤ ~2,080 actions at their pace). The bot is faster
  than the human upper bound, as a bot should be, and the two agree on the order of magnitude. v1 said a planner
  never makes one in 14 days; that described v1's planner.
- **The gate is rank, not skill.** The focused bot's Star-Metal Ark lands at 1,019 actions, just after rank 6 opens
  the Star Well at 1,004 — the Moon Sickle binding is the wall.
- **What comes after the Living Relic is not measured.** The next content is the floors (descend, restore, Keeper's
  Renown — docs/ROOMS.md), and no bot descends. So the long gap after it is the Antechamber's tail for that player,
  not the game's runway. A descending policy is the next probe story.

- **An engaged patron-minded player is on time for the pass and the album, and early for the Rush** (day 2.6 of 4,
  target not before day 3).
- **A casual 12 min/day player misses both**: the pass in all 12 runs, and the Rush top milestone in all 12. That is
  RV-D6's (#275) question, and on this build the answer is no.
- A player who ignores patrons (as the Board did) gets neither pass nor Rush: both are fed by orders.

## Gold

Banked at day 14 / earned over 14 days: planner engaged 137,810 / 588,760; planner casual 57,575 / 253,433; focused
engaged 97,600 / 418,591; greedy engaged 33,417 / 33,267 (it never upgrades a generator). Gold piles up for everyone
but greedy; nothing in the bots' reach spends it after the generators max out.

## Blind class

Floors 2-10 (bots never descend); anything bought (gems, chests, passes); daily quests, the wheel and the VIP crate
(they key off the real UTC day, which the simulation does not advance); opening the free chests the bots are given;
rewarded ads; and every human but one. The greedy baseline is deterministic, which the probe checks each turn; a
baseline that is deterministic and badly chosen would still skew density, and nothing here can see that.
